Oreneta Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

Oreneta is a desktop application. It has no server of its own, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. Your mail, calendars, contacts, notes and credentials stay on your computer and travel only to the servers of the accounts you add, and to the few services listed below. Each of those is named here with what it receives, and all but two of them happen only when you turn them on or ask for them.

What Oreneta stores, and where

  • Mail, calendars, contacts, tasks, notes and chat are kept in a local database on your computer, so the app works fast and offline.
  • Credentials (passwords, OAuth tokens, app passwords) are kept in your operating system's keychain, or in a local encrypted file where no keychain is available.
  • Backups you make, by hand or on a schedule, are encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you choose. They never include your account passwords or tokens.
  • Deleting an account deletes its local data; deleting a profile can delete all of that profile's data.

Meeting links (Meet, Zoom, Teams and others) are only recognised in event text and opened in your browser; Oreneta does not contact those services itself. A Google Meet link is created through Google Calendar only when you tick it.

If you set a proxy, connections for your accounts, feeds, file links and translation downloads go through it. Signing in, finding server settings, the update check and download, and pictures shown in messages do not use it yet.

Your operating system's web view (for example Microsoft Edge WebView2 on Windows) may make connections of its own, under its vendor's policy.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Oreneta requests access to Gmail (to read and send your mail), your basic profile (to label the account with your name, address and picture), Google Calendar (to show, create, edit and delete the events you ask it to) and your contacts, read only (to suggest addresses).

Oreneta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the mail, calendar and contact features you see, on your own computer.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train models, and no human other than you reads it.
  • It is never transferred to anyone, with one exception that you control: if you set up the assistant with a remote provider and run it on a Gmail message, the parts you reviewed and confirmed are sent to that provider, for that request only. Nothing is sent without that confirmation, and the assistant is off until you set it up.

Revoking access

Remove an account in Oreneta's settings and its local data is deleted. You can also revoke Oreneta's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to your Microsoft account at account.live.com/consent/Manage (work and school accounts: myapps.microsoft.com).
